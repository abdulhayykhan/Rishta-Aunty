# 🧕 Rishta Aunty — Desi & Pakistani Cultural Slang Lexicon

This guide is an encyclopedic, humorous dictionary of the Roman Urdu, Hindi, and Desi cultural slang terms, tech tropes, and matchmaking idioms baked into **Rishta Aunty**.

It provides context for international developers, hackathon judges, and open-source enthusiasts who want to appreciate the layers of satire embedded in the application.

---

## 1. Core Matchmaking Terminology

### 🧕 Rishta Aunty (رشتہ آنٹی)
- **Literal Translation:** Matchmaking Auntie.
- **Cultural Meaning:** The ubiquitous, all-knowing neighborhood auntie in South Asian society who maintains an unwritten dossier on every eligible bachelor and bachelorette. She scrutinizes bank balances, family lineage, skin tone, domestic habits, and culinary skills.
- **Developer Metaphor:** In our app, Rishta Aunty replaces GitHub recruiters and senior tech leads, evaluating your domestic worth through your Git commit history and open-source contributions.

### 📜 Biodata (بائیو ڈیٹا)
- **Literal Translation:** Matrimonial Curriculum Vitae.
- **Cultural Meaning:** A formal, ornate physical profile circulated between South Asian families outlining a candidate's age, height, skin complexion, education, salary, and family tree.
- **Developer Metaphor:** The centerpiece dossier generated by the app, replacing corporate resumes with domestic evaluations (*"Public Repos = Dahej"*, *"Stars = Mohallay ke Shashkay"*).

### 🏛️ Gotra / Baradari (برادری / گوتر)
- **Literal Translation:** Clan, sub-caste, or ancestral lineage.
- **Cultural Meaning:** Traditional lineage groups often prioritized during arranged marriage negotiations.
- **Developer Metaphor:** The candidate's technical lineage based on their primary programming ecosystem (e.g. *"Node_Modules Baradari — JavaScript Brahmins"*, *"Pure Monolithic C Baradari"*, or *"Tailwind CSS Rangrez"*).

### 📦 Dahej (جہیز)
- **Literal Translation:** Dowry / Marriage assets.
- **Cultural Meaning:** Material goods, property, and cash traditionally exchanged or presented during weddings.
- **Developer Metaphor:** The candidate's public GitHub repositories and open-source assets (*"Kitna maal jama kiya?"*).

### ⭐ Khandani Izzat (خاندانی عزت)
- **Literal Translation:** Family honor and generational prestige.
- **Cultural Meaning:** The social capital and respect a family commands within their community.
- **Developer Metaphor:** The candidate's GitHub stargazer count. If you have zero stars, your family honor is zero!

## 2. High-Frequency Slangs & Expressions

### 🎭 Topi Drama (ٹوپی ڈرامہ)
- **Literal Translation:** Cap theater / Feigned performance.
- **Meaning:** Pretentious posturing, excuses, or fake behavior designed to deceive others.
- **In-App Usage:** Used in the domestic habits section (*"Ghar Ke Kaam Ya Sirf Topi Drama?"*), roasting candidates who write 2000-word README files with fancy animated badges for projects where the actual code doesn't even compile.

### 👑 Phannay Khan (پھنے خان)
- **Literal Translation:** A legendary self-proclaimed big shot.
- **Meaning:** Someone who acts like they are the king of the world or an untouchable master, despite having modest or non-existent achievements.
- **In-App Usage:** Used in titles like *"Khali Haath Phannay Khan"* (A candidate acting like a principal software architect while having 0 public repos and zero commits).

### 🤦‍♂️ Chuss (چُھس)
- **Literal Translation:** A flat, nonsensical flop or lame joke.
- **Meaning:** A low-effort, disappointing action that drains the energy from the room.
- **In-App Usage:** Roasting bad commit messages (*"Commit messages mein 'fix bug', 'chuss', aur 'asdasd' likhta hai"*).

### ✨ Shashkay (شوشکے / ششکے)
- **Literal Translation:** Flashy show-offs, flair, or extravagance.
- **Meaning:** Flaunting non-essential aesthetic flourishes to impress onlookers.
- **In-App Usage:** Subtitle under the submit button (*"Kundali Nikalo — Full Shashkay"*), and referring to fancy GitHub profile README widgets, animated SVG graphs, and vanity metrics.

### ❌ Scene Off Hai (سین آف ہے)
- **Literal Translation:** The scene is cancelled / lights out.
- **Meaning:** A total failure, cancellation, or hopeless situation. The deal is dead.
- **In-App Usage:** The red rubber stamp slammed onto low-scoring candidates (*"SCENE OFF HAI ❌ — Phupho Ne Rishta Cancel Karwa Diya"*).

### 💖 Scene Full On Hai (سین فل آن ہے)
- **Literal Translation:** The scene is 100% active and rocking.
- **Meaning:** Everything is going wonderfully; massive success is guaranteed; celebrations are in order!
- **In-App Usage:** The green stamp for high compatibility scores (*"SCENE FULL ON HAI — Mithaai Baanto!"*).

### ☕ Chai aur Code (چائے اور کوڈ)
- **Literal Translation:** Tea and Coding.
- **Meaning:** The quintessential Pakistani developer diet. Code is never written without a steaming cup of doodh patti chai.
- **In-App Usage:** Official competition series theme for **GDG Live Pakistan Episode #2**.

### 🍵 Biryani Mein Elaichi (بریانی میں الائچی)
- **Literal Translation:** Cardamom pod hidden inside biryani.
- **Meaning:** The single, agonizingly unpleasant surprise that ruins an otherwise perfect experience when you accidentally bite into it.
- **In-App Usage:** Describing annoying technical red flags (*"Code review mein unexpected runtime error jaise biryani mein elaichi"*).

### 👵 Phupho Ke Taane (پھپھو کے طعنے)
- **Literal Translation:** Paternal aunt's sarcastic remarks.
- **Meaning:** The legendary biting sarcasm of a Pakistani father's sister (*Phupho*), known for finding flaws in even the most accomplished candidates.
- **In-App Usage:** Loading screen quote (*"Aunty is consulting with Phupho regarding your commit gaps..."*).

### 🏆 Sharma Ji Ka Beta Tier (شرما جی کا بیٹا)
- **Literal Translation:** Sharma Ji's legendary overachieving son.
- **Meaning:** The mythical neighbor's child who always scores 100%, gets the best job, and whom every Desi parent compares their disappointing children to.
- **In-App Usage:** The highest tier in the compatibility meter (*"Sharma Ji Ka Beta Tier — Scene Full On Hai!"*).
